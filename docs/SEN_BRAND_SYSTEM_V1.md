# Sēn — Brand System V1

## Brand idea
Sēn should feel like a premium operating layer for a richer offline life.

## Brand traits
- Warm
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Natural
- Premium
- Human
- Precise

## Avoid
- Neon fitness aesthetics
- Fintech dashboards as the dominant visual language
- Coupon marketplace cues
- Hyper-gamification
- Luxury that feels cold or exclusionary
- Generic wellness stock imagery

## Core palette
- Cream: #F6F0E6
- Soft cream: #EEE5D8
- Espresso: #402D23
- Deep espresso: #2D211B
- Ink: #201B17
- Muted olive: #7B7F62
- Sand: #DFCFBB
- Off-white card: #FFFAF2

## Typography
- Brand/editorial: elegant serif
- Utility/product: clean sans-serif
- Product hierarchy must prioritize readability over fashion.

## Photography direction
- Natural light
- Real architecture
- Real premium venues
- Warm materiality
- Movement without aggressive fitness clichés
- People should look like they are living, not posing for an ad

## Motion
- Slow enough to feel calm
- Fast enough to feel competent
- No confetti for routine actions
- No casino-style urgency

## Copy
Short, direct, slightly editorial.

Examples:
- More life. Less friction.
- What would make today feel better?
- A slower evening.
- Premium demand. Without public discounting.
- Brasília, deliberately.

## Product UI rule
Backend complexity should feel invisible. Member-facing states stay simple:
- Included
- Member Price
- Signature
- Privilege
- Drop
- Boost
