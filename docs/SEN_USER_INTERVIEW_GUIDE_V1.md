# Sēn — User Interview Guide V1

## Interview rule
Do not pitch for the first half.

The goal is to understand reality before asking for reaction.

## 25-minute structure

### 1. Current behavior — 8 min
- What did you do for fitness/wellness/work outside home last month?
- Which memberships/subscriptions do you pay for?
- Roughly how much did you spend?
- How do you decide where to go?
- What becomes annoying when your week gets busy?

### 2. Problem depth — 5 min
- Tell me about the last time you wanted to do something but gave up because of logistics.
- Have you ever paid for multiple memberships at the same time?
- What do you underuse today?

### 3. Prototype — 7 min
Give tasks without explaining UI.

Observe:
- navigation
- language
- expectation
- errors
- hesitation

Never say “click here.”

### 4. Pricing — 3 min
Show plan architecture.

Ask:
- Which would you choose?
- What makes it worth that?
- At what price would you stop considering it?
- What would make you cancel in month one?

### 5. Commitment — 2 min
Ask:
“If the launch network had the places you actually use, would you want to be considered for the Founding 25?”

Record behavior, not politeness.
