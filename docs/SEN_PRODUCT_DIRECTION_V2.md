# Sēn — Product Direction V2

## Strategic change
Sēn is no longer modeled as "membership required to enter."

The product becomes:
1. A useful free Sēn account.
2. A personalized operating layer for real life.
3. Membership as a contextual upgrade that becomes economically and experientially obvious through use.

## Free account
A non-member can:
- complete personalization onboarding
- browse Explore
- use Sēn AI
- discover Circles/Squads
- book eligible open-access experiences at normal/memberless price
- see potential savings
- see locked/member-only benefits
- receive contextual membership recommendations

## Membership
Membership should appear later, when it solves a visible problem.

Examples:
- "You booked 3 studio visits. Core would have saved R$ X."
- "This class is R$ 78 today. Core includes eligible visits like this."
- "This Privilege is available with Core."
- "Your pattern suggests Plus would fit better than Core."

The goal is not to hide membership.
The goal is to make the user understand why it is valuable before asking them to buy.

## Onboarding
No plan selection in onboarding.

Instead: 8-question personalization flow covering:
1. desired outcome
2. preferred time/routine
3. movement preferences
4. recovery interest
5. work/study habits
6. social preference
7. distance tolerance
8. current monthly spend

Output:
- personalized Home
- first useful recommendation
- no paywall

## Conversion philosophy
Sēn should convert through:
- usage
- savings
- access desire
- convenience
- privileges
- AI intelligence

Not through:
- forced signup walls
- immediate plan selection
- artificial urgency
- confusing credits

## UX bar
The visual/product bar should feel closer to the best consumer software:
- extremely refined
- calm
- high trust
- motion with purpose
- excellent typography
- spatial clarity
- premium materiality
- near-zero visual clutter
- no "template/AI-generated app" feeling

The design target is not "a wellness app."
It is a category-defining consumer product.

## Business implication
This creates a freemium funnel:

Free account → meaningful use → visible savings/access gap → Core/Plus/Black conversion

Key metrics:
- free activation
- first useful action
- first booking
- 30-day free retention
- membership recommendation exposure
- recommendation-to-plan-view rate
- plan-view-to-paid conversion
- savings-driven conversion
- privilege-driven conversion
