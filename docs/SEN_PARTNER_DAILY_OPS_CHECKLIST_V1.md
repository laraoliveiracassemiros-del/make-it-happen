# Sēn — Partner Daily Operations Checklist V1

## Before day starts
- confirm known closures
- confirm schedule/inventory sync
- verify sold-out/changed slots
- check partner cancellation notices
- test critical booking/check-in path when needed

## During day
- watch booking not recognized
- watch overbooking
- watch late partner changes
- resolve member issues before debating liability
- log all exceptions

## End of day
- completed bookings count
- no-shows
- partner cancellations
- manual check-ins
- refunds/credits
- unresolved issues
- payout-impacting exceptions

## Weekly
- reliability trend
- capacity used
- member feedback
- economics
- operations friction
- keep / adjust / pause partner status
