# Sēn — Go-to-Market Kit V1

## 1. Positioning
Sēn is a premium membership for real life that combines access to high-quality places and experiences, a natural AI coordination layer, real-world community, and transparent value.

### Short version
**One membership for more of real life.**

### Core value equation
**Access + AI + Community + Value**

### What Sēn is not
- Not a discount marketplace.
- Not a gym pass clone.
- Not a social feed.
- Not a bank.
- Not a clinical health platform in V1.
- Not a nationwide breadth play at launch.

---

## 2. Launch story
Sēn launches in Brasília with density before breadth.

Primary launch clusters:
- Lago Sul
- Asa Sul
- Sudoeste / Noroeste
- Asa Norte

Secondary only if supply quality is strong:
- Lago Norte
- Jardim Botânico
- Águas Claras

Target pilot network: **30–50 excellent partners**.

---

## 3. Member messaging

### Hero
**More life. Less friction.**

### Supporting line
Move, recover, work, discover and organize your week through one calm membership.

### Proof pillars
1. **Explore** — premium places that fit your real schedule.
2. **Ask Sēn** — natural AI that plans, finds, books and explains.
3. **Do things with people** — Circles and Squads designed for real activity.
4. **See your value** — clear visibility into what your membership is doing for you.

### Key differentiator
Sēn is not only about where you can go. It is about helping you actually use your life better.

## 5. Founding 25 positioning
The Founding 25 are not beta testers receiving a free app. They are the first members helping prove the complete Sēn experience.

What they receive:
- curated launch network;
- real booking access;
- Sēn AI planning/support;
- Privileges;
- direct founder feedback channel;
- founding pricing subject to pilot terms.

What Sēn needs from them:
- real usage;
- honest willingness-to-pay;
- candid feedback;
- tolerance for a concierge/manual operating layer during the pilot.

---

## 6. Partner proposition

### Core partner message
**Premium demand without public discounting.**

### One-sentence pitch
Sēn helps premium venues monetize selected capacity and acquire qualified recurring customers without forcing public discounting or unlimited low-value access.

### What partners get
- qualified demand;
- controlled inventory;
- incremental revenue;
- qualified member acquisition;
- distribution inside curated discovery;
- data and insight;
- community/event opportunities;
- operational support.

### What Sēn does not ask for
- unlimited access at unsustainable pricing;
- large pilot minimum guarantees;
- public discounting that damages brand;
- broad contact-data access to Sēn members.

---

## 7. Pilot structure for partners
Recommended pilot:
- 60–90 days;
- limited inventory;
- selected plan eligibility;
- usage-linked economics;
- test-booking before member launch;
- no large guarantees;
- weekly operational review early on.

Preferred commercial models:
1. Active member fee
2. Capped check-in
3. Hybrid activation + check-in
4. Pay per check-in
5. Dynamic inventory
6. Member co-pay
7. Sponsored acquisition

---

## 8. Initial user acquisition channels
Priority:
1. Founder network / warm introductions
2. Partner member overlap
3. High-intent local communities
4. Referral from Founding members
5. Curated local content / Instagram
6. Events / launch experiences

Avoid paid acquisition until activation, retention and booking reliability are understood.

---

## 9. Validation questions
Member:
- Would you pay for this at Core / Plus / Black pricing?
- Which category makes Sēn essential?
- Which category is nice-to-have?
- What do you already pay monthly?
- What would make you cancel?
- Does AI materially reduce friction?
- Do you understand Included vs Member Price vs Privilege?

Partner:
- Which capacity can you release?
- What inventory is most valuable to protect?
- Which commercial model feels acceptable?
- What member behavior creates operational pain?
- What payout level feels incremental vs cannibalizing?
- What data/reporting matters?

---

## 10. Launch rule
Do not scale because the brand looks good.

Scale only when:
- booking success is high;
- partner reliability is high;
- member repeat usage exists;
- support is manageable;
- contribution margin remains healthy;
- users can explain why Sēn is worth paying for.
