## Pilot go/no-go
GO when:
- member demand threshold met
- core flows usable
- supply density credible
- economics viable
- ops manageable

PAUSE when:
- interest is mostly aesthetic
- partner economics are structurally broken
- users need founder explanation to understand product
- support load is too high for cohort size
