# Sēn — Partner Outreach V1

## Goal
Start conversations with the strongest Brasília P0 partners only after the prototype and partner materials are ready.

## Outreach sequence
1. Warm introduction where possible.
2. Short founder message.
3. Send partner page / one-pager.
4. 20–30 minute discovery call.
5. Understand capacity and economics before proposing terms.
6. Offer a 60–90 day controlled pilot.
7. Run diligence and test booking before launch.

## Initial founder message
Hi [Name] — I’m building Sēn, a premium membership launching in Brasília that combines curated access to fitness/wellness/work spaces with AI planning and booking.

The model is intentionally different from a discount marketplace: partners control the inventory they release, and our goal is to send qualified recurring demand without forcing public discounting.

We’re selecting a small founding network for a 60–90 day pilot and [Partner] is one of the places we specifically wanted to speak with.

Would you be open to a short conversation this week so I can understand how you currently think about incremental capacity and external membership partnerships?

## Discovery call questions
- Which times/services have incremental capacity?
- Which inventory do you never want discounted?
- Do you already work with Wellhub/ClassPass/other aggregators?
- Are there exclusivity or channel restrictions?
- What does a high-quality external member look like?
- What creates operational friction at check-in?
- What cancellation/no-show behavior is acceptable?
- What payout structure would feel fair for unused/low-demand capacity?
- Who handles finance/reconciliation internally?
- What would make you stop a pilot?

## What NOT to do
- Do not lead with “discount”.
- Do not promise volume.
- Do not promise unlimited access.
- Do not send a giant deck before discovery.
- Do not negotiate against yourself with high minimum guarantees.
- Do not hide the fact that Sēn is early-stage.

## Pilot pitch
We’d like to start with a small, controlled test:
- 60–90 days
- limited member cohort
- agreed capacity
- usage-linked payout
- clear booking/no-show rules
- no large guarantee
- weekly review early on
- expand only if both sides see value
