# Sēn — First 12 Member Tests V1

## Goal
Test whether a new user understands the Sēn product without founder explanation.

## Composition
Recruit 12 adults in Brasília with variation across:
- current wellness spend
- fitness frequency
- premium gym/studio familiarity
- neighborhood
- current memberships
- single-category vs multi-category use

Avoid recruiting only close friends or people who already know the idea.

## Session length
25–30 minutes.

## Protocol
### Part A — context, no pitch
Ask:
1. What did you spend on fitness / wellness last month?
2. Which places or services do you already use?
3. What annoys you about organizing this?
4. Have you ever paid for an app or membership that aggregates access?
5. What would make you switch?

### Part B — silent prototype test
Give only:
"You just downloaded Sēn. Use it like you normally would."

Tasks:
1. Finish onboarding.
2. Find reformer after 18h.
3. Book as Free.
4. Find your Wallet.
5. Find Flex Packs.
6. Explain Sēn Price vs Included.
7. Ask the AI for something cheap and nearby.
8. Change something Sēn remembers.
9. Find a Squad.
10. Compare Free vs Core.

Do not explain unless the participant is fully stuck.

### Part C — money
Ask:
- What do you think Sēn Free costs?
- When would you stop paying avulso and subscribe?
- Does Core at R$399 feel too low / fair / high?
- What would need to be true for you to pay today?
- Would you rather buy 5 passes or Core? Why?

### Part D — commitment
Strong signals:
- asks when it launches
- gives contact for pilot
- accepts paid founding slot
- leaves deposit
- introduces a partner
- wants to invite someone

## Thresholds
Before moving forward:
- 80%+ complete booking without founder help
- 80%+ correctly explain Free vs Included
- 70%+ understand Wallet / credits
- 70%+ understand Packs without confusing them with membership
- 40%+ qualified participants show serious willingness at provisional pricing
- 25%+ show commitment stronger than words

## Important
Beauty is not a validation metric.
